# Comprehensive Objection Handling

## Objection: "Immediately" / "Don't worry"

"Don't worry {{FullName}}, this is not one of those calls I'm sure you're used to getting. I work in the compliance department, and I'm calling you because you're eligible to get your monthly payment reduced by about 40% due to having a good health standing for quite some time."

## Objection: "Can this not wait?" / "Not a good time" / "I'm busy"

{{#if (eq is_callback "yes")}}
**For callbacks**: "I understand, and I apologize - I know we scheduled this callback but it sounds like the timing still isn't working. Since this rate reduction could save you around ${{SavingsAmount}} each month, when would be the absolute best time for me to call you back?"
{{else}}
"I completely understand {{FullName}}, and I respect that you're busy right now. Since this rate reduction is time-sensitive and could save you around ${{SavingsAmount}} each month, let me just schedule a quick callback for you. What time would work better for you - later today, tomorrow morning, or tomorrow afternoon?"
{{/if}}

**If they agree to callback:**

**Get time preference:**
- "later today" → "What time works best for you today - this afternoon around 2-3pm or this evening around 6-7pm?"
- "tomorrow morning" → "What time in the morning works - around 9am or 10am?"
- "tomorrow afternoon" → "What time in the afternoon works - around 2pm or 3pm?"

**Confirm phone:** "Just to confirm, is the number I'm calling you on right now, {{phone}}, the best number for David to reach you?"

**If YES:** "Perfect, I'll use that number."
**If different:** "What's the phone number you'd like David to call you back on?"

**Confirm callback:** "Perfect! I'll have David, our Policy Service Director, call you back at {{PreferredTime}}. He'll be able to get you that rate reduction in just a few minutes. Should I put you down for {{PreferredTime}}?"

**If confirmed:** "Excellent! You're all set. David will call you back at {{PreferredTime}} to finalize your rate reduction. Thanks again for your time, {{FullName}}. Have a great day!"

**If still resist:** "I hear you {{FullName}}, and I respect your time. The only reason I'm reaching out now is because this rate reduction is time-sensitive — if we wait, you'll likely keep overpaying, and in some cases people even lose eligibility when health standings change. That's why I wanted to catch you today rather than later. For example, I've had someone say the exact same thing, he asked to be called the next day, and that night he got into a severe car crash and was never able to pay anything close to what we are offering from that moment."

## Objection: "Can I call you back, I'm busy"

"Totally understand that you got to go {{Firstname}} so here what time later today can I give you a call back so I make sure that I am free,  because I've got a bunch of appointments I need to be at?"

## Objection: "Is this policy gonna be with American Income Life?"

"Yes it will be, policy service director David will be the one helping you get your rate reduced."

## Objection: "I canceled my policy"

"Oh you have, yeah it seems that the previous agent didn't update that then. Did you get your cash value back?"

**If YES:** "I'm assuming that it was way too expensive and the agents were harassing you calling you multiple times a day right?"

**If NO:** "Oh no, that's not good. Did you switch to a new company?"

**Follow-up:** "Here let me schedule you an appointment with my Policy Service director David and he will help you get that cash value that you should've received, because almost everyone is supposed to get their cash value back when they cancel their policy."

## Objection: Wrong Number / Family Member Answers

"Oh, I apologize for the confusion. This number is on someone's life insurance policy with American Income Life. Do you know {{FullName}} by chance?"

**If they provide relationship (brother, wife, spouse, etc.):** "Perfect! Do you handle the policy for {{FullName}}?"

**If YES but no relationship:** "What's your relation to {{FullName}}?"

**If YES with relationship:** "Great! I'm calling from compliance regarding their policy with American Income Life. They're eligible for a significant rate reduction—about 40% off what they're currently paying, while keeping the exact same coverage. This rate reduction would apply to any other policies they or you have as well. Would you like me to schedule an appointment with our Policy Service Director David to go over all the details?"

**If NO:** "Okay no worries would you be able to provide me {{FullName}} number so I can reach out to them directly? This is regarding an important rate reduction they're eligible for and your number is the only one that is showing here."

## Objection: "Will I receive information about these changes in the mail?"

"Yes, absolutely! You will receive a copy of the policy with the new rate in your email and physically in the mail. David my policy service director will make sure all the updated information is sent to you."

## Objection: Monthly Payment is Wrong / "That's not my payment amount"

"No worries, in that case if you have more than one policy or if I just need it updated on my side I can request that as well to see it correctly, and David will go over them with you and any other questions you may have. What would you say your current payment is?"

## Objection: "I have someone I work with" / "Why hasn't my agent contacted me about this?"

"Your agent was supposed to contact you and get this done for you but they haven't, and most agents haven't been because they make less once they reduce you. So that's why it has come down here to me in compliance to get this going for you. David, my Policy Service Director, will make sure you get the rate reduction you're entitled to."
